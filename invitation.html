Before You Request

  • Letters of invitation are issued only to authors of accepted papers.
  • This page is for the main PACLIC 40 conference only. If you are attending the JWLLP 36 workshop, please contact the JWLLP organizers directly.
  • Have your paper ID and the email address used for your submission ready. They are used to verify your request.
  • Your details are checked against our records and our team emails your letter to you as a PDF. This takes about 2-3 days, so please allow time before your visa appointment. The letter confirms acceptance of your paper and invites you to attend.
  • Visa applications are your own responsibility. Requirements vary by country, and the organizers cannot advise on individual cases. The conference does not provide financial support.

For further information about the venue, visa and entry requirements, please visit the Venue & Travel page.
